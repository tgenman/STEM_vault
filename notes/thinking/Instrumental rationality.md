> [!tip] Instrumental rationality
> Instrumental rationality is the practice of choosing and executing actions that are likely to achieve one’s goals, given one’s beliefs, constraints, and uncertainty.

[[Decision making]] concerns the choice between possible actions, while [[Agency]] concerns the capacity to carry them out. A [[Heuristic]] can provide an approximate method when complete analysis is too expensive or impossible.

Instrumental rationality evaluates means relative to chosen ends; it does not by itself determine which ends are worth pursuing.
