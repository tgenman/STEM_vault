> [!tip] problem solving
> **Problem solving** is the process of moving from a current state to a goal when the required sequence of actions is not known in advance.

It includes representing the problem and its constraints, generating and testing possible approaches, and verifying that the result satisfies the original conditions. [[Decision making]] selects among actions; problem solving also constructs candidate actions and representations.
