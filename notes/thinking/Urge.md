Немеделенное эмоциональное побуждение

==Urge== - An immediate, emotional drive; something one wants on a visceral level. Distinct from a [[Goal]], which is an explicit, long-term target. Urges may be “positive” or “attractive,” in that they draw one forward or promote a certain action, or they may be “negative” or “aversive,” in that they repel one or make a certain action less desirable and less likely.
