> [!tip] Epistemic rationality
> Epistemic rationality is the practice of forming and updating beliefs so that they accurately reflect reality.

CFAR uses _epistemics_ for models of how knowledge and understanding are acquired and communicated. In this vocabulary, good epistemics means beliefs and reasoning processes that are well justified and closely matched to reality.

It requires distinguishing the map from the [[Territory]], adjusting confidence when evidence changes through [[Bayesian Updating]], and checking whether stated probabilities correspond to observed outcomes through [[Calibration]].

Its aim is not certainty, but beliefs whose confidence tracks the available evidence.
