> [!tip] diameter of a set
> For a nonempty subset $A$ of a [[Metric space|metric space]] $(X,d)$, its diameter is
> $$\operatorname{diam}(A)=\sup\{d(x,y):x,y\in A\}.$$

The set $A$ is [[Bounded set|bounded]] exactly when its diameter is finite.
