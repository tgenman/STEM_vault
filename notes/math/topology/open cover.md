> [!tip] open cover
> An **open cover** of a subset $A$ of a topological space $X$ is a family $\mathcal{U}$ of [[Open set|open sets]] such that
> $$A \subseteq \bigcup_{U \in \mathcal{U}} U.$$

A **subcover** is a subfamily that still covers $A$. A finite subcover contains finitely many sets; existence of such subcovers is central to the definition of a [[Compact set|compact set]].
