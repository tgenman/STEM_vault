> [!tip] Occam's razor
> Among explanations that account for the same evidence, prefer the one that introduces fewer unnecessary assumptions or entities.

The principle is not a claim that the shortest explanation must be true. It is a rule for avoiding unsupported complexity; evidence can justify a more complex model. [[Multiplication of entities]] is the error of adding explanatory machinery that does no work.
