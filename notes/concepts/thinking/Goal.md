Goal - An explicit target or aim; something one wishes to achieve or bring about. Distinct from an _[[Urge]]_, which is an immediate, emotional drive. Goals may be “instrumental,” in that they aid the achievement of other goals (e.g. earning a college degree), or they may be “terminal” or “near-terminal,” in that they seem to be good in and of themselves (e.g. happiness).
