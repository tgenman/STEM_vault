> [!tip] power analysis
> A calculation connecting a study design, sample size, effect size, variability, significance level and the probability of detecting a specified effect.

It can determine the required sample size for a target power, or the attainable power or [[minimum detectable effect]] for a fixed design. State the test and assumptions before computing it.

Source: [NIST: sample sizes required](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc222.htm).
