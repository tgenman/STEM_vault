> [!tip] standard error of the mean
> The standard deviation of the sampling distribution of the sample mean.

For $n$ independent, identically distributed observations with finite variance $\sigma^2$,
$$
\operatorname{SE}(\bar X)=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}.
$$
Replacing $\sigma$ by the sample standard deviation $s$ gives an estimated standard error. SEM describes uncertainty of the mean, not the spread of individual observations.

Source: [NIST: sample-size formulas for a mean](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc222.htm).
