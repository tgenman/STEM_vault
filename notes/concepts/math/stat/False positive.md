> [!tip] false positive
> A positive decision when the condition being detected is absent.

## in hypothesis testing

Rejecting a true $H_0$ is a Type I error. A valid level-$\alpha$ test bounds its probability by [[significance level|$\alpha$]]; this is not the posterior probability that a particular rejection is wrong.

Source: [NIST: statistical tests](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section1/prc13.htm).
