> [!tip] sample
> The units or observations selected from a target population for a study.

How the sample is selected matters for inference; being a sample does not by itself imply random selection or representativeness. [[Sample mean]] and [[Sample variance]] are statistics calculated from the sample.

Source: [NIST: populations and sampling](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/ppc/section1/ppc134.htm).
