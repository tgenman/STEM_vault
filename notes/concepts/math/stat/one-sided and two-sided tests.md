> [!tip] one-sided and two-sided tests
> A one-sided alternative specifies one direction of departure from the null; a two-sided alternative allows both directions.

For example, alternatives to a reference mean $\mu_0$ are $\mu>\mu_0$, $\mu<\mu_0$, or $\mu\ne\mu_0$. Tail probabilities and rejection regions follow the chosen alternative.

Source: [NIST: tests for a population mean](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc22.htm).
