> [!tip] hypothesis testing
> A statistical decision procedure that uses data to decide whether to reject a specified [[Null hypothesis H_0|null hypothesis]] in favour of a specified [[Alternative hypothesis H_1|alternative]].

The model, test statistic, [[significance level]] and rejection rule define the test. Not rejecting H₀ does not establish that it is true. Evaluate [[statistical power]] against a specified alternative.

Source: [NIST: statistical tests](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section1/prc13.htm).
