> [!tip] affine coordinate system
> An origin $O$ in a finite-dimensional affine space together with an ordered basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ of its translation vector space.

The coordinates of a point $P$ are the unique scalars $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ satisfying
$$
\overrightarrow{OP}=\sum_{i=1}^n x_i e_i.
$$
The basis need not be orthogonal or normalized. Calling every affine coordinate system “oblique” would exclude an allowed orthogonal special case.
