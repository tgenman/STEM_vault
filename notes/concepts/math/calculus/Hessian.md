> [!tip] Hessian $f''(x)=\nabla^2 f(x)$
> For $f\in C^2(U)$ on an open $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, the Hessian is the matrix of second partial derivatives:
> $$[\nabla^2 f(x)]_{ij}=\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_i\,\partial x_j}(x).$$
