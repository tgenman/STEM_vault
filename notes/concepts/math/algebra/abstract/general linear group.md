> [!tip] general linear group
> For a field $F$ and $n\ge1$, $\mathrm{GL}_n(F)$ is the group of invertible $n\times n$ matrices over $F$, with matrix multiplication.

The identity is $I_n$. Products remain invertible because $(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}$, and each member has its matrix inverse.

The set of all square matrices is not this group: it also contains singular matrices. Over a field, membership is equivalent to $\det A\ne0$.
