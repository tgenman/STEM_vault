$$
\sin 2 \alpha = \sin (\alpha + \alpha) = 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha
$$
$$
\cos 2 \alpha = \cos (\alpha + \alpha) = 1 - 2 \sin^2 \alpha = 2\cos^2 \alpha - 1
$$
