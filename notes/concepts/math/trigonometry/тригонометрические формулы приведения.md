$$\cos(x) = \sin(\frac{\pi}{2} - x) = - \sin(x - \frac{\pi}{2}) = \sin(x+ \frac{\pi}{2} )$$
$$\sin(x) = \cos(\frac{\pi}{2} - x) = \cos(x - \frac{\pi}{2} )  = -\cos(x + \frac{\pi}{2} )$$
$$\cot(x) = \tan(\frac{\pi}{2} - x) $$
$$\tan(x) = \cot(\frac{\pi}{2} - x) $$
$$\csc(x) = \sec(\frac{\pi}{2} - x) $$
$$\sec(x) = \csc(\frac{\pi}{2} - x) $$
