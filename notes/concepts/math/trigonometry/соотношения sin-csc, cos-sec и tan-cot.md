$\sin(\theta) = \frac{1}{\csc(\theta)}$, $\csc(\theta) = \frac{1}{\sin(\theta)}$,
$\cos(\theta) = \frac{1}{\sec(\theta)}$, $\sec(\theta) = \frac{1}{\cos(\theta)}$,
$\tan(\theta) = \frac{1}{\cot(\theta)}$, $\cot(\theta) = \frac{1}{\tan(\theta)}$.
