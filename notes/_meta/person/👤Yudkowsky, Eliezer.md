# Eliezer Yudkowsky

Eliezer Yudkowsky is an AI researcher and writer, a co-founder of the Machine Intelligence Research Institute, and the founder of LessWrong. His writing focuses on artificial intelligence, AI alignment, decision theory, and human rationality.

## selected works

- [Rationality: A-Z](https://www.lesswrong.com/rationality) — an edited collection of his essays on human rationality, also published as *Rationality: From AI to Zombies*.
- [Harry Potter and the Methods of Rationality](https://hpmor.com/) — a work of rationalist fiction.
